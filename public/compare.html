Venn compared with the tools you know.

Thinking of switching? Here's how Venn stacks up for finding a date for a group: what it does well, and where the others do better.

Comparisons

  • Doodle alternativeFree, with no ads, and nobody signs up. Not even you.See the comparison →
  • When2meet alternativeAsk when people are free, with big buttons that work on a phone.See the comparison →
  • Calendly alternativeFor finding a time with a whole group, not booking one-on-one meetings.See the comparison →

And everything else

There are plenty of good ways to schedule: a shared calendar, a booking link, or just asking in the group chat. Venn is for one job, finding the day a group can meet, and it stays free, with no ads and no accounts, for everyone.
