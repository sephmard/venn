How to schedule a book club meeting

  1. Pick the book first. Give people three to five weeks to read it, then ask about dates near the end of that.
  2. Offer three or four evenings. The same weekday each month is easy to remember; offer a couple of others as backup.
  3. Let people say "if needed". Someone who can make it but would rather not is different from someone who can’t. Venn shows both.
  4. Pick the night. Everyone adds it to their calendar from the poll, and nobody has to scroll back through the chat.

Tips for a book club that keeps meeting

  • Put the book in the note

    The title, the chapters to read, and whose turn it is to host.

  • Plan the next one at the end

    Send the next poll the week after you meet, while everyone is keen.

  • Mix up hosting

    Add the address in “Where”, or a video link for members who join from afar.

  • Copy last month’s poll

    Duplicate your poll from its organizer tools, change the dates, and share again.

Questions

How often should a book club meet?

Most meet once a month, which leaves time to read a book of about 300 pages.

Can members join by video?

Yes. Put a video link in “Where” and everyone sees it on the poll, and in their calendar once the date is picked.

Does everyone need an account?

No. People open the link, type their name and tap their answers. Nobody signs up or installs anything.

Is Venn free?

Yes. Venn is free and open source, with no ads and no tracking.
