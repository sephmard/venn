How to pick a Friendsgiving date with friends

  1. Start early. Send the poll three or four weeks ahead, before people book trips home. Late October or the first week of November is ideal.
  2. Offer three to five dates. The weekend before Thanksgiving is the favorite. The Friday and Saturday after catch friends who stay in town, and a weeknight can work for people who live close.
  3. Let people say "if needed". Everyone marks the days they can make, or can make if needed, so you can see which day is a stretch and which is simply out.
  4. Pick the date and lock it in. Once a day wins, pick it in Venn. Everyone can add it to Google, Apple or Outlook Calendar in a tap.

Tips for a smooth Friendsgiving

  • Settle the host first

    The host's kitchen and calendar set the limits, so only offer dates that work for them.

  • Plan the potluck in the note

    The template adds a note asking everyone to say what they're bringing. Edit it to suit your crowd.

  • Mind the travelers

    Friends flying home for Thanksgiving are often away from the Wednesday to the Sunday of that week.

  • Keep it to one link

    Share the poll in the group chat. Nobody signs up or installs anything.

Questions

When should Friendsgiving be?

Most groups choose the weekend before Thanksgiving. In 2026, Thanksgiving is Thursday, November 26, so Saturday, November 21 and Sunday, November 22 are popular picks. The weekend after works too, for friends who stay in town.

How far ahead should I send the poll?

Three to four weeks ahead gives everyone time to answer before travel plans are set.

Does everyone need an account?

No. Friends open the link, type their name and tap their answers. Nobody signs up, and there are no ads.

Is Venn free?

Yes. Venn is free and open source, with no ads and no tracking.
