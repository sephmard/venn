How to schedule a study group

  1. Work back from the exam. Offer a few times in the week or two before it.
  2. Share one link. In the class chat or by email. Nobody needs an account.
  3. See the best time. Venn puts the time that works for the most people at the top.
  4. Pick it. Everyone adds it to their calendar, with the room or video link in it.

Tips for study sessions that help

  • Say what you’ll cover

    Put the chapters or problem sets in the note.

  • Book the room after

    Once you know the time and the head count, book a library room.

  • Meet online if you need to

    Add a video link in “Where” for anyone studying from home.

  • Plan two sessions

    One early to cover the material, one just before the exam to review.

Questions

Is it free for students?

Venn is free for everyone, with no ads and no tracking.

Do classmates need an account?

No. They open the link, type their name and tap their answers.

Can we meet online?

Yes. Put a video link in “Where”.

What about different time zones?

Everyone sees the times in their own time zone.
