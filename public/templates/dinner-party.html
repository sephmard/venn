How to find a date for dinner with friends

  1. Offer three or four evenings. Enough to find one that works, few enough that everyone answers quickly.
  2. Share one link. In the group chat or by email. Friends tap the evenings they can make.
  3. See the best night. Venn puts the evening that works for the most people at the top.
  4. Pick it and add it to calendars. Book the table once you know the night and how many are coming.

Tips for a dinner everyone shows up to

  • Book after the date

    Once you know the night and the head count, booking a table is easy.

  • Say who’s cooking

    Potluck, restaurant or your place? Put it in the note.

  • Hide answers if you like

    Under Privacy options, only you see who said what.

  • Mind dietary needs

    Ask in the note, so nobody has to bring it up.

Questions

How many dates should I offer?

Three or four evenings in the next two or three weeks works well.

Can I see who can come, not just how many?

Yes. Tap a date to see who said yes or if needed.

Does everyone need an account?

No. People open the link, type their name and tap their answers. Nobody signs up or installs anything.

Is Venn free?

Yes. Venn is free and open source, with no ads and no tracking.
