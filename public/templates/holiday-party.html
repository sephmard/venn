How to choose a holiday party date

  1. Send it by mid-November. Calendars fill up quickly once December starts, so ask four to six weeks ahead.
  2. Offer weeknights too. A Thursday can beat a packed Saturday, especially for a work party.
  3. Let people say "if needed". Yes, if needed or no for each evening shows which night is easy for most people, not just possible.
  4. Pick the date and share it. Pick the winning night in Venn, and everyone can add it to their calendar from the poll.

Tips for a holiday party people actually come to

  • Stay clear of the last week

    The week of Christmas is when people travel. Aim for the first two or three weekends of December.

  • Say what kind of party it is

    Potluck, ugly sweaters, or just drinks? Put it in the note so nobody has to ask.

  • Keep work polls low-pressure

    Under Privacy options you can hide names from guests, so people answer honestly.

  • Book the place after the date

    Once you know the night and roughly how many can come, booking a room is easy.

Questions

When is the best time for a holiday party?

The first two weekends of December are the most popular, before people leave for the holidays. In 2026 that is Friday, December 4 to Saturday, December 12.

How far ahead should I plan?

Send the poll four to six weeks ahead. By early December most weekends are already taken.

Can I use Venn for a work holiday party?

Yes. You can hide names or everyone's answers from guests, and ask for emails, under Privacy options when you create the poll.

Is Venn free?

Yes. Venn is free and open source, with no ads and no tracking.
