Privacy

Last updated November 1, 2026

Venn is built to know as little about you as possible. You don't need an account, there are no ads and nothing follows you around. This page explains exactly what is stored.

What we store

  • Polls: the title, place, note, organizer name, time zone and proposed dates and times.
  • Responses: the name each person types, their answers, and the one-line note they add, if any. If the organizer hides names or answers, other guests don't receive them (or anyone's note) at all; only the organizer does. Notes are never emailed or sent to AI.
  • Your email, if you give it with your answer (it's optional, unless the organizer asks everyone for one): it's stored with your answer and shown only to the organizer, so they can invite you from their calendar once they pick the date. When they do, Venn uses it to send you that one email, with calendar links. On a vote, you can also give it after answering, to hear what's decided: one email when the organizer picks (one more each time they change their mind, at most three). Leave the field empty and it isn't kept; clear it later (Turn off) and it's gone. It's deleted when you remove your answer or the organizer deletes the poll.
  • Invited guests' emails, if the organizer sets a deadline and lists who they invited: kept with the organizer's private settings, shown only to them, and used only to remind those who haven't answered yet. Every reminder has a one-click link to stop them. They're deleted with the poll.
  • Who a poll emailed today: so that one poll can't email more than 100 different people a day, Venn stores each address it emails for the poll as a hash (SHA-256), not as the address. These are deleted after two days, or with the poll.
  • Notification settings, if the organizer turns them on: an email address or a webhook URL. These are never shown to people answering the poll.
  • Your account, only if you sign in (it's optional): your name, your email and the id Google, Microsoft or GitHub gives Venn for you, and which polls and brand templates you keep in My polls. Nothing else is asked for: no picture, contacts or calendar. Signed in, your browser holds one cookie for the session, which lasts 30 days or until you sign out. Delete your account on My polls and it's gone; your polls stay, with their links.
  • Secrets: only a SHA-256 hash of each organizer and edit link secret. The secret itself is in the part of the link after #, which browsers never send to the server.

What we don't collect

  • No passwords, and no sign-up needed to make or answer a poll.
  • No tracking cookies, tracking pixels, third-party analytics or advertising.
  • No calendar data. Check my calendar runs in your browser. Google and Microsoft are asked only for busy/free times, the sign-in token never reaches our server, and calendar files are never uploaded.

Anonymous usage counts

To learn what to improve, Venn counts how it's used, one number per day: for example, how many polls were created to vote on dinner times, or how many people answered a poll. Nothing about you or your poll is stored with those numbers: no names, titles, links, emails, IP addresses or device details, and nothing that connects one count to another. If your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track, nothing is counted.

Your browser saves which polls you organized or answered (in local storage on your device) so you can come back to them. You can clear it at any time in your browser settings.

Who processes data

  • Cloudflare hosts the site and the database.
  • Google, Microsoft or GitHub, only if you choose to sign in with them: they tell Venn your name and email.
  • Cloudflare Email (or Resend) sends emails: notifications, only to the address the organizer gave; copies of an answer, only to the address the person gave; the picked date or what a vote decided, only to guests who gave their email; and reminders, only to the guests the organizer invited.
  • Photon, run by Komoot with OpenStreetMap data, suggests places as an organizer types in Where. What's typed there is sent to it through Venn's server, so it never sees your IP address, and Venn doesn't log it. A Venn server can use Google Places for this instead, the same way, or switch it off, and then nothing is sent.
  • Anthropic runs Claude, the AI behind the optional AI buttons, only when you press one. What's sent depends on the button: what you typed to describe a poll or your organization; for an organizer's summary or message, the poll's title, times, and guests' names and answers; for Answer for me, what you typed and, only if you tick the box, when your calendar is busy (never what's in it). It goes through Venn's server, so Anthropic doesn't see your IP address. Anthropic doesn't train on it and deletes it within 30 days, unless it's flagged for a safety review. Venn counts AI requests each week, under a code made from your account or IP address that changes every week, and deletes the counts after that week. If you add your own Anthropic API key, it stays on your device and is sent only with your AI requests; Venn never stores it. AI isn't offered on polls inside other websites or on white-label polls.

We never sell or share poll data with anyone else.

How long we keep it, and how to delete it

Polls are kept until the organizer deletes them, or until a year has passed since anything happened with them: since the poll was made, last answered, its date picked and its last day or option. Then the poll is deleted with its answers, emails and notes. The organizer can delete the whole poll or anyone's response. Anyone can remove their own response. Every notification email has a one-click unsubscribe link.

Contact

Questions or requests? Use the form on the home page. What you write, and your email if you give it, is sent to us in one email and isn't stored by Venn. You can also open an issue, or for anything sensitive, follow our security policy.

Build on Venn. It's open source, with a free API and polls you can put on your own website.
