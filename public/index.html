How it works

Three steps. One link.

  1. 1

    Propose a few times

    Pick days on a calendar and add times, or open a range and let people paint when they're free.

  2. 2

    Share one link

    People type a name and tap Yes, If needed or No. No app, no account, great on a phone.

  3. 3

    Pick the overlap

    Venn ranks every time by who can make it. Choose one and everyone can add it to their calendar.

Our promise

No accounts. No ads. No tracking.

  • Time zones, handled

    Everyone sees the times in their own time zone, automatically.

  • Check your calendar

    Fill in answers from Google, Outlook or a calendar file. It stays in your browser.

  • Know when people answer

    Get an email, or a message in Slack, Discord or any webhook.

  • Open source

    MIT licensed. Read the code or run your own.
