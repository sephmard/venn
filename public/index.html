Find the time that works for everyone.

Dinners, game nights, trips. Share one link and see when everyone's free. No sign-up.

How it works

Three steps. One link.

  1. 1

    Suggest dates

    Pick a few dates to vote on, or ask when people are free.

  2. 2

    Share one link

    Everyone taps Yes, If needed or No. No account needed.

  3. 3

    Pick the winner

    Venn ranks every option. Pick one and everyone adds it to their calendar.

Our promise

No accounts. No ads. No tracking.

  • Any time zone

    Everyone sees times in their own zone.

  • Your calendar

    Fill in answers from Google or Outlook, in your browser.

  • Instant updates

    Email, Slack or Discord when someone answers.

  • Two kinds of poll

    Vote on dates, or find when people are free, even days in a row.
