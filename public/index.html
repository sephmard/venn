How it works

Made for the phone in your hand.

  1. 1. Make a poll

    Name the plan and tap a few evenings. No account needed.

    Yes, if needed or no for each option. Nothing to install.

For groups

Less group chat. More plans.

  • No sign-ups, for anyone

    Friends open the link, type a name and tap. Nothing to install.

  • Dates, or free days

    Vote on a few dates, or let everyone mark when they're free, even days in a row for a trip.

  • See who's in at a glance

    The best date rises to the top, with everyone who can make it.

  • Lock it in

    Pick the date and everyone adds it to Google, Apple or Outlook Calendar.

  • Never chase replies

    Get an email, or a Slack or Discord message, when someone answers.

For developers

Open source. Built to build on.

MIT licensed, no framework and no build step. Call the API, wire up webhooks, or run your own Venn for free.

  • JSON API

    Create polls, collect answers and pick the date over plain HTTP.

  • Signed webhooks

    Every answer can post to Slack, Discord or your own endpoint.

  • Self-host for free

    Deploy to Cloudflare Workers and D1, or any server with Node.

  • Private by design

    Edit links carry secrets the server only stores as hashes.

Questions

What is Venn?

Venn is a free scheduling poll for groups. Suggest a few dates, or ask when people are available, share one link, and see the date that works for everyone.

Is Venn free?

Yes. Every feature is free, with no ads and no paid plan. Venn is open source.

Do people need an account?

No. Not the organizer, and not the guests. People open the link, type their name and tap their answers.

How is Venn different from Doodle or When2meet?

Venn has no ads and no accounts, lets people answer "if needed", works with whole days, parts of the day or hours, and is made for phones. When the date is picked, everyone can add it to their calendar.

Does Venn work across time zones?

Yes. Everyone sees the times in their own time zone.
