How it works

1

Suggest some dates, or ask when people are free

Have a few evenings in mind? Tap them on the calendar and pick the times. Planning a trip? Choose a range of days and let everyone mark when they're free. Venn even finds a few days in a row that work.

2

Share one link

Drop it in the group chat. People type their name and tap Yes, If needed or No for each option. No app to install, and it's made for phones.

3

See the overlap and pick

Venn ranks every option by how many people can make it, then by how many gave a firm yes. Pick one, and everyone gets links to add it to Google, Outlook or Apple Calendar.

Everything you need, nothing you don't

  • No accounts

    Nobody signs up, not even the organizer. Your link is your key.

  • Time zones handled

    Everyone sees the times in their own time zone, automatically.

  • Check your calendar

    Fill in your answers from Google Calendar, Outlook or a calendar file. Only busy/free times are read, in your browser.

  • Know when people answer

    Get an email, or a message in Slack, Discord or any webhook, when someone responds.

  • Days, evenings or whole trips

    Vote on specific evenings, find a free day, or find three days in a row for a weekend away. All-day plans go into calendars as all-day events.

  • Open source

    MIT licensed. Read the code, suggest a feature, or run your own copy on Cloudflare or any Node server.

Private by design

No cookies, no analytics, no ads. Venn stores only what people type into a poll. Edit links carry a secret the server never sees, and the organizer can delete the poll at any time.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Venn is open source and cheap to run, so there's no paid plan, no trial and no ads.

Do the people I invite need an account?

No. They open the link, type a name and answer. That's it.

How do I edit my poll later, or from another device?

When you create a poll, your browser remembers that you're the organizer. To manage it from somewhere else, copy your private organizer link from the poll page and keep it safe.

Can Venn see my calendar?

No. Check my calendar runs in your browser and only asks Google or Microsoft for busy/free times. The sign-in never reaches our server, and calendar files are never uploaded.

How long are polls kept?

Until the organizer deletes them. People can also remove their own answers at any time.

Can I run it myself?

Yes. Deploy it free on Cloudflare in a few minutes, or run it on any server with Node. See the self-hosting guide.
