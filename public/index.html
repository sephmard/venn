Why Venn

Less group chat. More plans.

No more "which week?" threads. Everyone marks their free days once, and the day that works for the whole group is right there, with everyone who can make it.

The good stuff, for everyone.

  • Free, always. Every feature, for everyone. No paid plan.
  • No sign-up. Not for you, not for your friends.
  • No ads, no tracking. Just your plans.
  • Open source. MIT licensed, on GitHub.
  • Made for phones. Opens from the group chat, in any browser.
  • Any time zone. Everyone sees the times in their own.
