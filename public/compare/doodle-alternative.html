Where Doodle shines

Doodle is a mature scheduling product. It connects to your calendar, and it does more than group polls: booking pages for one-on-one meetings and sign-up sheets, which suit people who schedule at work all week.

Venn does one thing. It finds the day a group can meet, for dinners, book clubs, trips and other get-togethers, and keeps that free and simple for everyone.

How to switch in 1 minute

  1. Open Venn. Go to venn.day/new. There's no account to make.
  2. Choose Vote on a few dates. It works like the polls you know: name the plan and tap the dates or times you'd like to offer.
  3. Share the link. Paste it in the group chat. Friends answer yes, if needed or no for each option.
  4. Pick the date. The best one rises to the top. Pick it, and everyone can add it to their calendar.

Questions

Is Venn really free?

Yes. Every feature is free for organizers and guests, and there are no ads. Venn is open source, so anyone can read the code or run their own copy.

Do I or my guests need an account?

No. Nobody signs up, not even the organizer. Guests open the link, type their name and tap their answers.

Can people answer if needed?

Yes. Everyone marks each option available, if needed or unavailable, so you can tell a stretch from a no.

Does Venn handle time zones?

Yes. The organizer sets times in their own time zone, and everyone sees them in theirs.

Can I bring my Doodle polls over?

There is nothing to import. Finish the polls you have where they are, and start your next one on Venn. It takes about a minute.
