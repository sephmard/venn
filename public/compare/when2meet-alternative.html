Why groups pick Venn

  • Made for phones

    Guests answer one day at a time with buttons big enough for a thumb, or tap mornings, afternoons and evenings. No dragging, no zooming.

  • Yes, if needed or no

    People can say a time is a stretch, so you can tell the time that suits everyone from the one they'd squeeze in.

  • Two kinds of poll

    Ask when people are free, or suggest a few dates and let everyone vote.

  • Into everyone's calendar

    Once you pick the time, everyone can add it to Google Calendar, Outlook or Apple Calendar in a tap.

  • Time zones handled

    You set times in your time zone, and everyone sees them in theirs.

  • Free, open source, no sign-up

    No ads and no accounts. Your organizer key is kept on your device, and Venn's server stores only a one-way hash of it.

Where When2meet shines

When2meet is free, needs no sign-up and is wonderfully simple. Its grid shows the whole group's availability at a glance, and at a desk with a mouse it's quick to fill in. If your group is happy with it, keep using it.

Venn is for the times people answer from their phones, want to say if needed, or need the final time in their calendars.

How to switch in 1 minute

  1. Open Venn. Go to venn.day/new. There's no account to make.
  2. Choose Ask when people are free. Say how long the plan is, then mark the days and hours guests can choose from.
  3. Share the link. Paste it in the group chat. Everyone marks when they're available, from any phone or computer.
  4. Pick the time. The best times rise to the top. Pick one, and everyone can add it to their calendar.

Questions

Does Venn work on a phone?

Yes. Venn is designed for phones first: big buttons, one day at a time, and no dragging across a tiny grid. It works on a computer too.

Can I ask when people are free, like on When2meet?

Yes. Choose Ask when people are free, mark the days and hours guests can choose from, and share the link. Guests pick mornings, afternoons and evenings, or exact times.

Is Venn free, with no sign-up?

Yes. Venn is free, with no ads, and nobody signs up, not even the organizer. It is open source too.

What does if needed mean?

It means a time works, but it is not ideal. Venn ranks times by how many people can make them, and when two are tied, the one with more clear yeses comes first.

What happens once we find a time?

The organizer picks it in Venn, and everyone can add it to Google Calendar, Outlook or Apple Calendar in a tap.
